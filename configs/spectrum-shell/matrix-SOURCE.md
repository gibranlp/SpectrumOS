# Matrix rain attribution

The glyph atlas `matrix-code-msdf.png` is `assets/matrixcode_msdf.png` from
[Rezmason/matrix](https://github.com/Rezmason/matrix), revision
`5ba90490453ceceb6812d6b1bc658a99a92411d0`, licensed under the accompanying
`matrix-LICENSE.txt` (MIT, copyright 2018 Rezmason).

The native Qt shader adapts the repository's MSDF glyph reconstruction and
organic timing ideas from `shaders/glsl/rainPass.frag.glsl` and
`shaders/glsl/rainPass.raindrop.frag.glsl`. The atlas uses 57 symbols in an
8 × 8 grid, 512 × 512 pixels, with an MSDF pixel range of 4. No browser,
network access, upstream intro, or logo sequence is used at runtime.
