# Igor of the Week

Star Trooper Igor won poll #3. Package 0.3.5 adds him permanently to the free wardrobe and schedules automatic selection for November 16–22, 2026 (local date), using `2026-W47-star-trooper`. This PC selects him early for testing. His visor pulses and scans, with accent colors and shoulder plates behind the paws.

The package applies `star-trooper.patch` and six explicitly listed PNGs from the private Igor pack to the verified 0.3.4 free source archive. No other private outfits are included. `free-runtime.json` identifies the unchanged upstream archive; the package and resulting runtime version are 0.3.5.

Detective Igor is Igor of the Week #2, following Winter Igor (#1). The Sunday-night November 8 update prepares his automatic selection for November 9–15, 2026 (local date). The wardrobe refreshes every minute, so sessions left running across midnight also activate him.

Each featured outfit is selected once per user during its week, including users with a previous manual selection. The saved `activatedWeek` marker preserves subsequent outfit choices. Detective uses `2026-W46-detective`; Winter retains `2026-W45-winter` for November 2–8. Loading the update from November 16 onward does not activate Detective. Disabled Igor visits remain disabled.

Star Trooper, Detective and Winter remain permanently free, without an account or network connection. The free installation includes Original Igor, Day of the Dead, Christmas hat, Christmas lights, Winter Igor, Detective Igor and Star Trooper Igor. Other designs remain excluded from the free runtime export allowlist.

Open the wardrobe with **Super + Control + I**.

The 0.3.4 runtime explicitly draws the holding paw above the magnifier. Its enlarged eye fades with Igor’s eyelid closure, including naps, so sleeping Igor never shows an open eye through the lens.
