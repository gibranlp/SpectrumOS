# Igor of the Week

Winter Igor is the first Igor of the Week and joins the permanently free outfits. It can be selected without an account, including offline. The update selects Winter once per user when the wardrobe first loads during November 2–8, 2026 (local date). The saved `activatedWeek` marker prevents later refreshes from replacing the user’s subsequent choices. Updates first loaded from November 9 onward do not change the selection. Winter remains free after the featured week. This release does not force-enable disabled Igor visits.

The free installation includes Original Igor, Day of the Dead, Christmas hat, Christmas lights and Winter Igor. Other new designs remain private and excluded from the free runtime export allowlist.

Open the wardrobe with **Super + Control + I**.
